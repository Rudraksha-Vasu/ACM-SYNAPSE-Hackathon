    iHow PhishGuard works+

    Paste a website address or an incoming email, then select Analyze. PhishGuard checks the supplied text in your browser and explains the indicators it finds.

    1. Inspect the URL

    We identify the destination hostname and check for embedded usernames, selected brand lookalikes, IP addresses, HTTP, short links, and unusual ports. Submitted links are never opened.

    2. Inspect the email

    We look for requests for sensitive information, pressure tactics, sender/domain inconsistencies, and links in the message. The Email message tab checks incoming content; Email drafter helps you review an outgoing message and create a draft to copy into your email app.

    3. Explain the score

    Each matched rule adds indicator points, capped at 100. Scores of 0–24 are low concern, 25–59 medium concern, and 60–100 high concern. The score is not a probability.

    4. Verify in context

    Review the evidence and contact the organisation through a known channel when something looks suspicious. Pasted text cannot authenticate a sender, and a low score does not establish safety.

    This prototype uses explainable rules. It does not visit destinations, inspect attachments, query live reputation services, or use a trained machine-learning classifier.
